Tests using 3C418

This website explores the impact of source structure corrections on VLBI astrometric results using offsets extracted from VieVS.

Four experiments are available. The East experiment introduces a secondary component 1.5 mas west of the core, while the North experiment introduces a secondary component 1.5 mas north of the core. The third introduces a secondary component at the point 1.5,1.5 and the fourth experiment introduces a second component at the point -1.5,-1.5.
